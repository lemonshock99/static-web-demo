Typography is the art of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed. In branding, it's a silent ambassador for your company.

### The Voice of Your Brand

Is your brand playful or professional? Modern or traditional? The font you choose is a key part of that voice. A serif font like Garamond can feel classic and reliable, while a sans-serif like Helvetica feels clean and modern.

### Key Considerations:

*   **Readability:** First and foremost, can your audience read it easily across all devices?
*   **Scalability:** Does the font look good at all sizes, from a tiny footnote to a giant headline?
*   **Personality:** Does the font's character align with your brand's values?

Choosing a font is a strategic decision that should not be taken lightly. It impacts user experience, brand perception, and the overall effectiveness of your communication.
