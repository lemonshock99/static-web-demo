Minimalism isn't just about what you remove; it's about what you leave behind and why. In web design, this philosophy translates to creating clean, uncluttered, and highly functional user experiences.

### 1. The Power of Whitespace

Whitespace, or negative space, is the most critical element in minimalist design. It gives content room to breathe, reduces cognitive load, and helps guide the user's eye to the most important elements on the page. Don't be afraid of empty space; embrace it.

### 2. Limited Color Palette

A minimalist design typically sticks to a monochromatic or very limited color palette. We've chosen a palette of ivory, black, and gold for this very reason. It creates a sense of harmony and elegance without overwhelming the user.

### 3. Purposeful Typography

When visual elements are sparse, typography takes center stage. A well-chosen font can convey personality, improve readability, and act as a design element in its own right. We use 'Inter' for its clean lines and modern feel.
